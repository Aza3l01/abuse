# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (template)

chartered accountant or counsel before first use. Points to confirm are listed at the end.

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on [DATE] between:

1. **Clew Technologies Private Limited** (CIN U62090KL2026PTC104122), a company incorporated in India with its
   registered office at Idukki, Kerala, India ("Clew"); and
2. **[COUNTERPARTY NAME]**, [a company incorporated in / an individual resident of] [JURISDICTION], with its
   address at [ADDRESS] (the "Counterparty").

Each is a "Party" and together the "Parties". Each Party may be a "Discloser" or a "Recipient" depending on who
discloses information.

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to exchange confidential information to evaluate or carry out [DESCRIBE THE PURPOSE, for example:
a possible purchase of Clew's service, a Clew Audit engagement, a partnership or an investment] (the "Purpose").

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by the Discloser to the Recipient, in any form, in
connection with the Purpose, that is marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be
confidential given its nature and the circumstances. It includes, for Clew, source code, detection logic,
models, prompts, architecture, security findings, pricing not published, business plans and customer
information, and for either Party, any personal data and any log data shared.

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Recipient can show:

a. is or becomes public through no breach of this Agreement;
b. was lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure;
c. is lawfully received from a third party with no duty of confidence; or
d. is independently developed without use of the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 4. Obligations

The Recipient will:

a. use the Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
b. keep it confidential, with at least the care it uses for its own confidential information and never less than
   reasonable care;
c. disclose it only to its employees, officers, advisers and contractors who need to know it for the Purpose and
   who are bound by duties of confidentiality at least as protective as this Agreement, and remain responsible
   for their compliance;
d. not copy it except as needed for the Purpose, and not reverse engineer any software, model or sample provided;
e. tell the Discloser promptly after becoming aware of any unauthorised use or disclosure.

## 5. Compelled disclosure

If the law, a court or a regulator requires the Recipient to disclose Confidential Information, the Recipient
will, where lawful, give the Discloser prompt notice so that it may seek protection, and will disclose only
what is legally required.

## 6. Personal data

Neither Party will share personal data under this Agreement unless it is needed for the Purpose. Any personal
data shared will be handled in line with applicable data protection law, including India's Digital Personal
Data Protection Act, 2023.

## 7. Return and deletion

On the Discloser's written request, or when the Purpose ends, the Recipient will within 30 days return or
delete the Confidential Information and confirm that in writing. The Recipient may keep copies that the law
requires it to keep, or that sit in routine backups, and those stay confidential under this Agreement until
deleted.

## 8. No licence and no warranty

All Confidential Information stays the property of the Discloser. No licence or other right is granted except
the right to use it for the Purpose. It is provided "as is", without any warranty. Neither Party has to
disclose any information or to enter into any further agreement.

## 9. Term

This Agreement covers disclosures made during the [2] years from the date above. Each Recipient's duties for a
piece of Confidential Information continue for [3] years after it is disclosed. For source code, detection logic
and personal data, the duties continue for as long as the information remains confidential or the data is held.

## 10. Remedies

Unauthorised use or disclosure may cause harm that money alone cannot repair, so the Discloser may ask a court
for an injunction or other equitable relief, in addition to any other remedy.

## 11. General

a. This Agreement is the whole agreement on its subject and may be changed only in writing signed by both
   Parties.
b. Neither Party may assign it without the other's written consent.
c. If part of it is unenforceable, the rest continues.
d. It may be signed in counterparts and electronically.
e. It does not create a partnership, agency or employment relationship.

## 12. Governing law and disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The courts at Idukki, Kerala have exclusive jurisdiction.

## Points for the CA or counsel to confirm (not part of the agreement)

1. **Stamp duty.** NDAs executed in India may need stamping under the state stamp act. Confirm the duty and
   the method for Kerala, and for an e-signed copy.
2. **Section 27, Indian Contract Act, 1872.** This draft has no non-compete and no non-solicit, so it should not
   engage section 27 (restraint of trade). Keep it that way unless counsel advises otherwise.
3. **Duration.** The 2 year disclosure period and 3 year confidentiality tail are placeholders. Confirm.
4. **Foreign counterparties.** Decide whether to keep Idukki courts or use arbitration (bracketed option above).
5. **Trade secrets.** Indian law has no standalone trade secret statute, so this contract and equitable remedies
   carry the protection. Confirm the remedies wording.
